SCOREBOOK · HARDWOOD

A test copy with two made-up teams. Your game stays on this device only — nothing is sent anywhere.

  1. Shots: drag a player onto the court. Drop on the bottom half = made, top half = missed; where you drop decides paint / 2 / 3.
  2. The basket counts right away. Then answer the question (ASSIST BY? / REBOUND?) or just skip it.
  3. Everything else: tap a player, then a button — FREE THROW, OFF REB, DEF REB, ASSIST, STEAL, BLOCK, TURNOVER, FOUL, TEAM REB.
  4. On the court: the centre circle is TURNOVER (top) / STEAL (bottom); the strip behind each backboard is BLOCK.
  5. Don't know who it was? Use the NO PLAYER tile and pick the player later in PLAY LOG.
  6. Quarter: tap the quarter box by the score; END OF PERIOD moves to the next one.
  7. Mistakes: UNDO LAST takes the last play out; PLAY LOG > FIX for older ones.
  8. SUB swaps players in and out. BOX SCORES and TEAM STATS show the totals. Menu > SETTINGS > NEW GAME starts over.
